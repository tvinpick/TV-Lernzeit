LERNZEIT – DEUTSCH ALPHABET IMAGE ASSETS (38 PNG)

All images are individual 600x600 PNG RGBA files with transparency.
Images have no embedded text; use word_de in ASSET_MANIFEST.json to render German names through Godot UI.
The original requested vocabulary contained 36 words, plus two bonus G words (Gans, Gabel) = 38 total.

Place PNG/ files under res://assets/deutsch/words/
Use asset_manifest for word lookup; the filenames are ASCII and case-sensitive.
Some items were carefully cut from an illustrated sprite sheet and scaled up; they do not have native 600px detail.
The letter groups should be unlocked according to parents' chosen sequence (not forced alphabetically).
Some stylized objects have friendly faces; they are illustrative, not literal photographs.

Visual quality note: the extra Gans/Gabel are clean vector-style bonus illustrations, slightly simpler than the glossy rendered characters.
